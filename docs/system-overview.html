Chief of Staff System

Five Grok bots and one Mac. You talk to the Chief. The other four each own one part of the work. Your Mac does everything that touches the repos, the board, transcripts or data, and it runs its own schedule. Nothing leaves the team, merges, or changes shared state without your yes on that exact action.

kit: ~/cos/kit (git) engines: Claude Code, Cursor Agent bots: Grok Bot

Two paths reach the Mac. When you ask, the Chief runs cos-run and you approve the card that shows the exact command. On a schedule, the Mac runs the job itself and hands the result to the bot that owns it. A bot never runs anything on your Mac while you're not looking.

A job that finds nothing new sends nothing. A job the Mac slept through is reported as missed, never replayed late with old data.

Three flows

A request

  1. You ask the Chief, in your own words.
  2. A quick lookup runs right away. Anything bigger comes back as a brief: task, done-means, constraints, lane, cost cap.
  3. You say go. The approval card shows the exact cos-run command. You tap Allow.
  4. The job runs, the checker reviews it, and the Chief reports: needs you, done, blocked.

A board write

  1. STEWARD's capture stages writes for your cards in apply.sh, previewed with DRY_RUN=1.
  2. The Chief shows you the batch and its sha256.
  3. You say yes to that hash. The card shows cos-run apply <job> <sha256>.
  4. Only that exact file runs, once. The Chief re-reads each card to confirm.

A correction

  1. You correct a bot. The Chief records it with cos-run note.
  2. Seen twice, it's a pattern. KEEPER finds it in Friday's audit.
  3. KEEPER proposes the strongest fix: a setup change, then a check, then a skill edit, then memory. A prompt change comes with its eval result.
  4. You approve and apply it. The kit's git history keeps every change.

The walls

Approval cardsEvery command on your Mac shows the exact command first. Mac execution is set to ask every time.
Custom RulesSending, posting, calendar, sheets, GitHub writes, purchases and Zoom sign-in always stop and ask.
The gateBlocks secrets, production writes, infrastructure, force-push, canon values and edits to the system's own controls in every job.
Approval by hashA write batch runs only if it matches the sha256 you approved, once.
The team's rulesdata-org and data-org-notebook come first: the writing standard, the boundary, context/ read-only, canon charter decision 14.
No mergesThe furthest an agent goes is a draft PR, after your yes. Owners merge.
